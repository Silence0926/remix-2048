Privacy Policy

2048 Remix Web

Data We Process

When online leaderboard is enabled, we may process nickname, score, game mode, grid size, steps, daily seed, timestamp, and a local client identifier generated in your browser.

Purpose

Data is used only to provide leaderboard ranking, basic abuse prevention, and service stability.

Storage

Leaderboard records are stored on the backend database configured by the service operator. Local gameplay state and preferences are also stored in your browser localStorage.

Retention

Leaderboard records may be pruned for moderation, abuse prevention, or storage limits. We do not guarantee permanent storage of all records.

Your Choices

You can play offline without cloud sync. You can also change nickname at any time. If you are operating this service yourself, you may delete records via admin cleanup APIs.

Contact

For public deployment, replace this section with your support email or contact page.
